# Achievement artwork v1 — 20 October 2026

All 19 configured achievements use custom Vybaa enamel badge art. A twentieth
signature badge handles unknown or future types. Existing type, milestone and
badgeIcon API/persistence fields stay compatible; images are resolved by type and
milestone, so old earned achievements receive the same artwork immediately.

Generated with the built-in image tool in two sheets of ten. Masters are in
`social-media/vybaa/source/achievements/2026-10-20/v1/` relative to the workspace.
Runtime WebP files and crop manifest are in `public/assets/achievements/v1/`.
Reproduce extraction with `scripts/pack-achievement-badges.mjs`.

Prompt shared by both batches:

> Exactly ten distinct polished 3D enamel collectible badges, regular 5-column
> by 2-row grid, wide 5:2 canvas. Each isolated on genuine transparency, centered
> in its equal square cell, generous padding, no overlap. Sculpted rounded metal
> and enamel medallions with dark charcoal structure, raspberry pink #d50053,
> warm gold #fac000, ivory highlights and restrained brushed silver. Soft studio
> shading, bold silhouettes at small size. Tiny original V-shaped notch motif.
> No emoji glyphs, words, labels, numbers, background or checkerboard.

Batch one, row-major: rising star; upright sword and shield; crossed swords;
winged shield; crown; double star; trophy; diamond infinity crest; target and
arrow; ribbon medal.

Batch two, row-major: flame crest; lightning; linked rings and crown; gauntlet;
star laurel; seven jewels and checkmark; phoenix; sunrise; crescent and star;
signature V flame.
