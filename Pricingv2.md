# Vybaa Pricing V2

## Free

The free plan should let people feel the core value of Vybaa before asking them to pay.

- 3 active goals
- Basic daily or weekly routines
- 1 Rewind session per day
- 7-day Rewind insights
- Basic goal progress and check-ins
- A small number of Rewind partners
- 1 owned community

## Vybaa Pro

Pro should unlock depth, continuity, personalization and scale.

- Unlimited active goals
- 30-day and 90-day Rewind insights
- More than 1 Rewind session per day
- Longer and richer Rewind conversations
- Full Rewind history with search
- Full Rewind partner library
- Goal history and analytics
- Completed goal archive
- Missed-day and recurring-blocker patterns
- Custom schedules and morning/evening routines
- Flexible check-in windows and multiple check-ins per day
- Goal pauses and rescheduling
- Up to 10 owned communities
- Community insights, templates and shared-goal tools
- Longer or richer voice Rewind sessions
- Personalized weekly reviews
- Reflection and goal-history export

## Recommended rollout order

1. Full Rewind history
2. Advanced goal analytics
3. Longer or more frequent Rewind sessions
4. More Rewind partners
5. Personalized weekly reviews
6. Voice Rewind limits
7. Export and sharing

## Product rule

The free experience must include a real goal, a real Rewind conversation and a useful small insight. Pro should make the experience deeper and more personal, rather than hiding the basic emotional value behind a paywall.

## Existing limits already implemented

- Free users can have 3 active goals
- Pro users have unlimited active goals
- Free users can access 7-day insights
- Pro users can access 30-day and 90-day insights
- Free users can own 1 community
- Pro users can own up to 10 communities
- Free users can use 1 Rewind routine session per day
- Pro users can use 2 Rewind routine sessions per day
- Morning/evening and custom Rewind routines require Pro
